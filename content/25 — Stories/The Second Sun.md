# The Second Sun

*Three stories of Japan, told by one boy across three lives.*

These stories are fiction. The teachings they touch come from the tradition.

The tradition has always taught through stories. The *Yoga Vasishtha* tells of Gadhi, who lived a whole lifetime in the moment he was under the water while bathing. These three stories use the same old device. A boy in Japan falls asleep and lives another life before morning.

They are set far from India. What happens in them happens in every home. A mother is lost. A father grows old and needs bathing. Food comes from machines, care is handed to strangers, and a family keeps the things it never says out loud.

## The three parts

- [[The Crease]]. Part I. A boy in Tokyo dreams he was born in 1923, and lives through Hiroshima to the night instant noodles were invented. About 18 minutes.
- [[The Fold]]. Part II. On his first night in Hiroshima the same boy dreams he is a banker, from the bubble years in Osaka to the last page of his working life. About 25 minutes.
- [[The Shape]]. Part III. The boy, grown up and awake in 2050, cares for his father in a Japan that a machine can switch off for a night. About 16 minutes.

Read them in order. Each part ends with a few threads into the practice, for anyone who wants to follow them.
