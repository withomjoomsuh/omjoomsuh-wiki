# Kundalini

## What it is

Kundalini is the latent spiritual energy that the yogic and tantric traditions locate at the base of the spine, in [[Chakras#The Seven Principal Chakras|Mooladhara]]. It is pictured as a serpent coiled three and a half times, asleep. Practice is said to wake it, and as it rises through the central channel, [[Nadis#The three principal nadis|sushumna]], it passes through the [[Chakras|chakras]] one by one until it reaches the crown. That rise is the tradition's picture of spiritual awakening.

## Sanskrit and etymology

*Kuṇḍalinī* (कुण्डलिनी) comes from *kuṇḍala*, a coil or ring, and also from *kuṇḍa*, a pit or bowl, the hollow in which the energy is said to rest. Kundalini is understood as a form of [[Shakti]], the feminine creative power, asleep in the individual body.

## Where it appears in the canon

The *Hatha Yoga Pradipika* opens its chapter on mudras with kundalini: as the serpent king supports the earth with its mountains and forests, so kundalini supports all the practices of yoga.[^1]

The *Sat-Chakra-Nirupana*, a sixteenth-century text by Purnananda, gives the classical description of the chakras and of kundalini's ascent through them. John Woodroffe translated it in 1919 as *The Serpent Power*, which brought the idea to Western readers.[^2]

[[Swami Satyananda Saraswati]]'s *Kundalini Tantra* sets out the Bihar School's approach: purification of the body and the nadis, preparation of the chakras, and only then practices aimed at awakening.[^3] The lineage's [[Surya Namaskara]] is taught as part of that preparation.

## What is contested

**Physical or symbolic.** Some teachers describe kundalini as a literal energy with physical effects. Others read the serpent, the channels and the centres as a symbolic map of how attention and vitality change in deep practice. Nothing in current physiology corresponds directly to it.

**Safety.** Teachers in this lineage and others warn that forcing awakening before the body and mind are ready can be destabilising. Gopi Krishna's 1967 account of his own difficult experience is the best-known description.[^4] The traditional safeguard is slow preparation under a qualified teacher.

## Why it matters

For most householders, kundalini stays on the horizon. The practices that prepare for it, steady posture, balanced breath, purification of the nadis and a settled mind, are worthwhile in their own right. The lineage's caution is the useful part: build the ground first.

## Related concepts

- [[Chakras]], the centres kundalini rises through
- [[Nadis]], the channels, especially ida, pingala and sushumna
- [[Shakti]], the power kundalini is a form of
- [[Prana]], the vital force
- [[Pranayama]], breath control that prepares the nadis
- [[Surya Namaskara]], a preparatory practice in this lineage
- [[Vama Marga]], the tantric path aimed at its awakening
