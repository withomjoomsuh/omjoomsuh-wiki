# Swasthya Kosha

## What it is

The Swasthya Kosha is a printed A5 journal published by OMJOOMSUH, kept by hand for a few minutes each morning. It calls itself a rhythm reset ritual. Its purpose is small and steady: to give a householder a regular place, with a pen, to return to her own rhythm before the day takes over.

It arrives inside the NEEV welcome kit and is also sold on its own, on Amazon.

## Sanskrit and etymology

*Svāsthya* (स्वास्थ्य) is the usual Sanskrit and Hindi word for health. Its root sense is *sva-stha*, established in oneself, standing in one's own place. *Kośa* (कोश) means a sheath, and also a treasury or store. The name can be read both ways: the health of the sheaths, or a store of health built up a morning at a time.

## The teaching it stands on

The journal is built on the lineage's map of the five sheaths, the [[Pancha Kosha]]. It attends to them in the order the lineage teaches, from the inside outward, and it opens with [[Manah Prasad|manah prasad]], the gladdening of the mind, because the lineage holds that the breath and the body both follow the mind.

Its daily practice moves through three steps: becoming steady, observing, and self-study, the last of which the tradition calls *svadhyaya*. The duty-to-self questions of the [[Four Dharmas]] sit inside it.

## What it is not

The journal makes no medical claim and is not a treatment for any condition. It is a habit of attention, kept on paper. A record in a person's own handwriting, built over weeks, is what it offers.
